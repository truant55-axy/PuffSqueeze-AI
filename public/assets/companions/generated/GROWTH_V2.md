# Growth v2: Explorer and Mature companion

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Transparent PNG atlases, eight animals
per stage, with live SVG eyes mapped in src/components/growthVisuals.ts.
The first connection stage continues to use the existing individual PNGs.

Files: growth-explorer-atlas-v2.png, growth-mature-atlas-v2.png.
Reference order: Puff bird, tabby cat, spaniel, pig; penguin, owl, gull, dolphin.
Earlier v1 art is retained as version history.

## Explorer prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new transparent sprite atlas for the EXPLORER juvenile stage of the eight PuffSqueeze companions. Input image is IDENTITY AND RENDERING STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: its animals are far too baby-like, so redesign their anatomy to be visibly older. Preserve soft softly shaded illustrated/clay finish and exact species/colors. ONE wide 2:1 transparent PNG, exactly FOUR equal columns x TWO equal rows of eight separate full-body animals. Each fits within the central 76% of its own equal square cell, with transparent gutters on all four sides. No text, grid, ground shadow, scenery or props. Order top row: white Puff bird in PLAIN powder blue shirt, blue-gray/cream tabby cat, tan/cream long floppy-ear spaniel dog, pink pig. Bottom row: navy/cream penguin, tan/cream owl, white/gray seagull yellow beak, light-blue/white dolphin. REQUIRED VISIBLE ANATOMY CHANGES: heads are only about 40-45% of total body height rather than the giant baby heads in reference. Longer visibly developed torsos, longer legs/paws/wings, more defined muzzles/beaks, developed tails, slimmer cheeks, subtler blush. Cat is youthful seated but longer-legged and tail lifted; dog is an adolescent spaniel sitting tall with longer muzzle and forelegs (recognizable dog, not monkey); pig is a juvenile pig STANDING naturally on four visible hooved legs with a longer horizontal torso and distinct pig snout, never upright human; penguin stands distinctly taller with longer flippers; owl has a defined layered chest and longer wing feathers; seagull has elongated body and partly open long wings; dolphin has visibly elongated hydrodynamic body, longer rostrum, pronounced dorsal/tail fins in a swimming diagonal. Puff keeps white curl feather and plain light-blue clothing, more pear-shaped taller torso, slightly spread wings, not a round infant. Small plain blue scarf/collar allowed but NO charms/crowns/leaves/badges. Faces gently smiling with BOTH eyes CLOSED in clean curved eyelids so we can animate eyes over them, no pupils, no thick extra eyebrows. Heads nearly frontal to show both eyes. Youthful companionship for teenagers, recognizable friendly animals with real growth silhouettes, never identical baby bodies with changed accessories. Large high quality atlas.

Reference: growth-bloom-atlas-v1.png, identity/rendering style reference only.
Source output: exec-4ffdfbe1-c0ea-40f1-ac24-4b72ac8027de.png.

## Mature prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Produce ONE new transparent sprite atlas for the final MATURE COMPANION stage of these exact 8 animals. Input is their ADOLESCENT stage; this output must look like a real large growth step from it. Maintain softly shaded illustrated finish, friendly expressions and matching species colors. Adult animals with clearly elongated bodies, heads just 28-35% of full body height/length, more defined anatomy, little or no blush, NOT cute oversized baby faces. Still warm and approachable, never frightening, no photorealism. Exactly FOUR equal columns x TWO equal rows, wide 2:1 PNG. Full bodies centered INSIDE each equal square tile with 13% CLEAR TRANSPARENT GUTTERS all around; no animal crosses tile boundaries. No text, scenery, props, grid, symbols, ground shadows, crowns, leaves, star badges. Same order: TOP 1 white Puff bird with signature forehead curl, taller pear-shaped torso, LONG wider developed feathered wings partly spread and fuller layered tail, plain LIGHT BLUE shirt with NO lettering, distinct avian proportions; 2 blue-gray cream adult tabby CAT standing on FOUR long natural legs, elongated back, distinct smaller cat head and long curved tail, head turned near-front to show two closed eyes; 3 tan cream adult SPANIEL DOG standing naturally on FOUR legs with a long athletic torso, mature muzzle, developed chest and long flowing floppy ears, NOT sitting like puppy reference; 4 pink adult PIG standing naturally on FOUR hooved legs, clearly larger horizontal torso, mature longer snout and ears and small curled tail, friendly graceful pig silhouette never humanoid. BOTTOM 1 mature navy cream PENGUIN upright with very tall streamlined torso, long flippers and smaller head, subtle graphite/slate feather shading; 2 adult tan cream OWL with tall developed torso, long layered feather wings semi-spread, defined round face smaller relative to chest, confident feet; 3 adult white gray SEAGULL in standing wings-out pose, long feathered wings and neck, yellow beak/feet, balanced within tile; 4 adult light-blue white-belly DOLPHIN swimming in a stretched diagonal, LONG streamlined body, long rostrum, very distinct dorsal fin/flippers/tail, graceful rather than a short round baby. SMALL plain blue collar/neckerchief permitted for identity only. Both eyes must be clean CLOSED smiling curved eyelids, NO visible pupils, no thick painted eyebrows, heads face sufficiently forward to see both eyes clearly. Preserve exact eight identities; massive body-proportion and posture change, not simply accessories or scaling. High-quality production sprite atlas, genuinely transparent background.

Reference: growth-explorer-atlas-v2.png, previous growth stage.
Source output: exec-fa8951df-8117-4759-870e-d65523ed4718.png.

Both outputs are 1774 x 887 pixels. Per-sprite SVG viewports preserve aspect ratio.
Some mature sprites extend beyond equal atlas cells; non-destructive SVG clipping
polygons isolate their silhouettes from neighboring sprites. PNGs retain their
original transparent output. Eye landmarks are defined in source pixel space
and transformed with the same contain fit as the artwork. The mature dolphin
has one visible eye because its head is shown from the side.
