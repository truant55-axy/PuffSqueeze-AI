# Puff companion and live animation

The built-in image generation tool produced `puff-bird-v3.png` on 2026-10-14. The original Puff SVG was rendered as an identity reference. The approved pig and dog images were style references. The new PNG preserves Puff's white body, light-blue plain clothing and sleeves, white hands, yellow feet and head tuft. Its original transparent alpha was preserved on copy into the project.

All eight companion base images contain closed eyelids. `HealingAnimal.tsx` draws aligned SVG eyes with highlights over each face. The eyes briefly close for 150ms at independently randomized 2.8–6.2 second intervals; pressing or greeting the companion also closes its eyes. The base artwork remains still while its SVG eyes and overall float/press transforms animate.

`FloatingCompanion.tsx` renders the selected companion on signed-in screens other than Home. Pointer capture supports mouse and touch dragging. Positions are bounded to the viewport and kept above the bottom navigation. Normalized positions are stored per user, so screen resizing and route changes preserve accessible placement. Arrow keys move the focused companion; the Home key or double-click returns it to its default corner.
